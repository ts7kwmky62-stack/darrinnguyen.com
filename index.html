How we work

Five rules every job runs on, for our own team and for yours.

  1. Updates at milestones

    You hear from us when something is done, at milestones we agree on up front. No chasing.

  2. Claims are checked

    A vendor's "it's compliant" or a tool's "it worked" is a claim until we see the document or the result.

  3. Done means tested

    Every deliverable has an acceptance test agreed before work starts. You sign off against it, not against "looks done".

  4. Written scope

    We do what was agreed. New ideas become a written change order you approve first. No surprise bills.

  5. Your data stays yours

    Client data stays locked to the job that brought it in. We don't reuse it, and we only use tools whose terms let us keep it out of model training.

How we hire

  1. One broad hire per division

    A general counsel before five lawyers. The head hire covers the whole department until the work outgrows it.

  2. Specialists earn their seat

    A specialist is hired only after three independent jobs need the same craft, or the head hire's instructions start to conflict with the work.

  3. The owner approves every hire

    No new seat, model or agent without the owner's OK.
